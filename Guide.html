Tips to finish any course you start!


  1. Set a fixed schedule and stick to it
    Treat the course like an appointment—decide when and where you'll study each week, and make it non-negotiable. Consistency builds momentum.

  2. Break it into small, clear goals
    Instead of saying “finish the course,” focus on micro-goals like “complete module 1 by Wednesday” or “watch 2 videos today.” This makes progress feel manageable and rewarding.

  3. Stay accountable
    Tell a friend about your goal, join a study group, or use a tracking app. Even a simple checklist can keep you motivated and give you a sense of accomplishment.
